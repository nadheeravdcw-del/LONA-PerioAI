Hybrid AI-Assisted Platform for Periodontal Charting with Image-Based Gingival Analysis


Periodontal assessment is a key component of dental diagnosis and treatment planning, traditionally performed through clinical examination and manual charting. While these methods are reliable, they can be time-consuming and may show variability in documentation between clinicians. At the same time, fully automated diagnostic systems are still limited in replacing clinical judgment. To address this, a hybrid platform has been developed that combines clinician-led periodontal charting with AI-assisted image analysis.

In this system, periodontal charting and clinical data entry are performed manually by the clinician, ensuring diagnostic control and flexibility. Intraoral images are used exclusively within the AI module to assist in the assessment of gingival parameters, particularly the measurement of attached gingival width. The AI component identifies relevant anatomical landmarks from the images and provides measurement support based on pixel-level analysis, which is then reviewed by the clinician.

The platform also supports structured documentation aligned with contemporary periodontal frameworks, including the European Federation of Periodontology (EFP) classification, enabling organized representation of periodontal status without replacing clinical interpretation.
The objective of this hybrid approach is to improve efficiency, enhance measurement consistency, and reduce documentation burden while maintaining clinician authority in diagnosis and decision-making. Future enhancements may include integration of radiographic data such as intraoral periapical radiographs (IOPA) to expand diagnostic support.

Overall, the system demonstrates how artificial intelligence can function as an assistive tool within a clinician-driven workflow, improving standardization without replacing clinical expertise.
